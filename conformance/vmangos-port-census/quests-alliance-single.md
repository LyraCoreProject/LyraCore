## Differences

- `quest_template`: selection keeps 4433 of 4727 source rows.
- `creature_questrelation`: selection keeps 3898 of 3908 source rows.
- `creature_involvedrelation`: selection keeps 4060 of 4069 source rows.
- `gameobject_questrelation`: selection keeps 262 of 262 source rows.
- `gameobject_involvedrelation`: selection keeps 209 of 209 source rows.
- `areatrigger_involvedrelation`: selection keeps 50 of 50 source rows.
- `mail_loot_template`: selection keeps 72 of 72 source rows.
- Quest templates and text follow selected giver relations in the profile's shared quest catalogue. Full profiles use the full catalogue; other canonical profiles share the starter and instance catalogue. Relations emitted into a Realm use local enabled spawn choices. S6 still owns EventAI dependency closure.
- Objective differences follow selected quest targets and items available from the catalogue's creature loot, chests and vendors. Forbidden items are removed. Unavailable collect targets and non-goober GameObject targets remain omitted and are counted in the manifest. Cast and area-trigger objectives follow their selected relations.
- Fixed and choice reward differences follow selected quest rows. Learn rewards use a single-effect learn wrapper from selected source spells. Other reward casts are unsupported.
- Mail differences follow quest-template mail IDs, delays and money. vmangos reads no classic-db quest-end script table. Used mail loot must be one unconditional item with 100% chance and no reference.
- Event requirements come from source exploration/event flags, except quests whose area-trigger objective already supplies that completion gate. Source script/event delivery remains for S6; the manifest reports those requirements. Classic-db derives its event requirements from the current EventAI plan.
- vmangos supplies explicit `RewXP`. The Module cannot distinguish zero XP from its fallback calculation; the manifest counts that limit, along with unrepresented prerequisites, scripts, flags and extra reputation fields.
