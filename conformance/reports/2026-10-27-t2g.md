## Tier 1 precision

The first tier 1 run had 98 records, including 79 extra splines and two false coordinate differences.
Default JSON parsing changed `52.476253509521484` to `52.47625350952149` and
`986.2713623046875` to `986.2713623046876` while loading the Golden Trace. The in-process candidate
retained the exact decoded f32 values. In the wire comparison both traces passed through the same
JSON parser, so those two false differences did not appear.

Core now enables `serde_json/float_roundtrip` in both consumers' dev-dependencies. A focused test
against the pinned Golden Trace failed before that change and passed afterward. No coordinate
tolerance was added, and no packet or comparison record was filtered. These parser defects are
fixed here and do not belong in the taxi gameplay gaps.
