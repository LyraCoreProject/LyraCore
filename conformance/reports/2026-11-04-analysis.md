### Evidence and uncertainty

**E1 — likely real passenger-combat gap.** The base taxi audit has 86 distinct rule signatures from 182 raw records, versus zero Tier 1 rule signatures. Of these, 79 are extra VALUES paths `field_46` through `field_124`: unit flags plus the full aura block. They are not 79 independent gameplay defects. At 44.5–67.4 seconds creatures repeatedly name passenger GUID 2 as victim; spell 6982 hits it at 66.653 seconds, followed by root/aura packets and an unroot at 71.164 seconds. Spell 3391 also names the passenger. This extends the rehearsal’s 27 raw records and the earlier attack/spell/flags symptom family; raw totals are not a regression metric. [module/src/creatures/cycle/ctx.rs:549](https://github.com/LyraCoreProject/LyraCore/blob/7fd8a7774942d0908fb05022737bd7a9d95be7be/module/src/creatures/cycle/ctx.rs#L549) constructs aggro candidates without taxi state, and [module/src/creatures/ai.rs:70](https://github.com/LyraCoreProject/LyraCore/blob/7fd8a7774942d0908fb05022737bd7a9d95be7be/module/src/creatures/ai.rs#L70) only excludes dead/godmode targets. [module/src/creatures/cycle/mod.rs:755](https://github.com/LyraCoreProject/LyraCore/blob/7fd8a7774942d0908fb05022737bd7a9d95be7be/module/src/creatures/cycle/mod.rs#L755) selects hostile nearby candidates, [module/src/native/combat.rs:131](https://github.com/LyraCoreProject/LyraCore/blob/7fd8a7774942d0908fb05022737bd7a9d95be7be/module/src/native/combat.rs#L131) emits engagement packets, and [module/src/native/aura.rs:41](https://github.com/LyraCoreProject/LyraCore/blob/7fd8a7774942d0908fb05022737bd7a9d95be7be/module/src/native/aura.rs#L41) emits a full aura mask. Reconnect field_46 timing (40.335s expected versus 42.115s; later 41.537s versus 61.863s) may include combat-state transitions rather than a flight scheduler defect.
